Method & sources. All launch data comes from TokenLaunched logs on the pons V2 factory (0x7eD5…EC7e), read directly from the official Robinhood Chain RPC (rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com) in your browser — the launch-rate chart is an exact count, not an extrapolation. In V2 a token starts on a bonding curve (no pool until graduation): the radar reads each curve's quote balance vs its per-launch graduation threshold, and graduations themselves are counted from the factory's graduation events — no heuristics. Burn figures are the PONS balance of 0x…dEaD plus 24h Transfer logs into it. Market data: DexScreener public API. Holder data: Blockscout API. Contract addresses from docs.ponsfamily.com/v2. The inspector also searches the retired V1 factories for legacy tokens.
Live refresh. Launch feed refreshes every 60s; market, holder, burn and radar data every 5 minutes; the full 24h scan every 30 minutes (and on "Refresh now"). If the RPC or APIs are unreachable from your network, the page falls back to the 28 Jul snapshot — the pill next to the title shows which mode you're in.
About pons V2. Fixed-supply (1B) launches now start on a bonding curve that holds the entire supply — the liquidity pool is created only at graduation, which eliminates pre-graduation liquidity sniping. Quote asset and graduation threshold are configurable per launch (most use native ETH with a 4.2 threshold). Graduation happens automatically inside the purchase that completes the curve; liquidity is then locked permanently. 80% of protocol fees still buy back and burn PONS.
Limitations. The radar shows progress for ETH-quoted launches (the large majority); token-quoted launches are still counted and their graduations still detected. Holder counts exclude the curve/pool, factories, lockers and burn addresses and require >1 token; on graduated V2 tokens the count may include the new pool until DexScreener indexes it. The safety score is a heuristic screen, not a contract audit; creator activity in the inspector covers the scanned 24h window (the public RPC's log index does not filter on the deployer topic, so deeper history is not queryable).
