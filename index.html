Method & sources. All launch data comes from TokenLaunched logs on the active pons factory (0xA5aA…1feB), read directly from the official Robinhood Chain RPC (rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com) in your browser — the launch-rate chart is an exact count, not an extrapolation. Graduation progress is the WETH balance of each launch pool vs the 4.2 WETH threshold. Burn figures are the PONS balance of 0x…dEaD plus 24h Transfer logs into it. Market data: DexScreener public API. Holder data: Blockscout API. Contract addresses and event signatures from docs.ponsfamily.com. The legacy factory (0x0c37…77a4) is near-inactive (~3 launches per 100K blocks) and is excluded from rate/feed; the inspector searches both factories.
Live refresh. Launch feed refreshes every 60s; market, holder, burn and radar data every 5 minutes; the full 24h scan every 30 minutes (and on "Refresh now"). If the RPC or APIs are unreachable from your network, the page falls back to the 28 Jul snapshot — the pill next to the title shows which mode you're in.
About pons. Fixed-supply (1B) token launches against WETH; pool fee 1%; graduation at 4.2 WETH; creator earns 70% of trading fees (90% on legacy launches); 80% of protocol fees are TWAP-bought back into PONS and burned. Community takeovers of abandoned tokens are possible via the official CTO process. pons reportedly accounts for over half of all Robinhood Chain transactions (The Crypto Times, 27 Jul 2026); a V2 with RWAs and ETH-denominated fees has been reported (BSCN, Jul 2026).
Limitations. "Graduated" is proxied via pool WETH balance vs the 4.2 threshold; holder counts exclude the pool, factories, locker and burn addresses and require >1 token; the safety score is a heuristic screen, not a contract audit; creator activity in the inspector covers roughly the last 28 hours of blocks.
Independent community project — not affiliated with pons or Robinhood. Nothing here is financial advice.
